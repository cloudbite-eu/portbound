Portbound

Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

No sign-up, no account, no advertising, no purchases and no tracking. The app sends no data about you to us.

Portbound (the app) is made by Lukasz Abramowicz ("we"), who is responsible for the data described here. This policy explains what happens to information when you play the app on iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

Your game, your trading house and your settings are stored on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. The app also keeps a copy of your game in your own iCloud (Apple's iCloud key-value storage), so a new iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account continues your game. Apple handles iCloud data under the Apple Privacy Policy; we cannot see it. Settings, Your data, Delete all removes your game from the iPhone and from your iCloud.

Play stats (optional)

If you are 16 or older and switch on Play stats in Settings, the app keeps a log of your play on your iPhone: when you play and for how long, and what happens in your game (deliveries, levels, the daily paper, whether you share your day). It carries a random number, not your name, and the app never sends it anywhere. You can see it, copy it or delete it in Settings, Your data. If you take part in a test and choose to e-mail it to us, we use it only to see how the game plays, keep it no longer than 13 months, and delete it sooner if you ask.

Game Center (optional)

If you are 16 or older and switch on Leaderboards in Settings, the app sends your scores and achievements to Apple's Game Center, where they appear under your Game Center profile. Apple handles this data under the Apple Privacy Policy. We do not receive your Apple Account, your e-mail address or your contacts. You can switch Leaderboards off in Settings at any time.

Notifications

If you allow them, the app schedules notes on your iPhone: the morning paper, only if you switch it on, and a ship coming home from a charter. They are made on your iPhone and nothing is sent to us. The widget and the Lock Screen show the game's market and your ships the same way.

The daily news file

Once a day the app downloads a public file with that day's news for the game from GitHub Pages. The request carries no information about you or your game. As with any web request, GitHub receives your iPhone's IP address and basic technical information to deliver the file; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not receive or store this information. Without the file, the game plays the days that came with the app.

Sharing and links

When you share your day, the app opens your iPhone's share sheet, and what you share goes only where you send it. Real news in the game names its source; tapping it opens the source in Safari, where that website's own privacy policy applies.

Under 16

If you tell the app you are under 16, Play stats and Leaderboards stay off and nothing in the game goes online. The app collects no data about anyone.

Your rights

We hold no data about you unless you e-mail it to us. For any question about privacy, or to have something you sent us deleted, write to hello@cloudbite.eu. If you live in the European Union, you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
