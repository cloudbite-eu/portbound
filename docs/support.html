Support

Questions, a bug, or an idea for the game? Write to hello@cloudbite.eu.

Please say which iPhone and iOS version you use and, for a bug, what you did just before it happened.

Where does the news come from?

Every day a file with that day's news is published for the game: a few real facts from public sources, each in the game's own words with its source named, and the game's own events, marked "Game event". Every player trades the same world on the same day.

Where is my game saved?

On your iPhone, and a copy in your own iCloud. A new iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account continues your game.

How do I start again?

Settings, Your data, Delete all. It removes your game from the iPhone and from your iCloud, and cannot be undone.

What is today's run?

One short game a day, the same for every player: a ship, 1,000 play coins and three trips, set where the day's lead story is. Every choice shows what it earns, and the run is scored out of 100 against the best plan, which the next day's paper prints. It does not touch your empire's coins, apart from its reward.

What is the morning paper?

A note at 8 in the morning on days with real news: the day's run and the story that set it. It is off until you switch it on in Settings, and the line under the switch says whether tomorrow has one.

What is a charter?

From level 6: a contract that takes one of your ships away for a few real hours and pays several of its usual trips. Lock your iPhone and the Lock Screen shows the ship on its way.

Why can't I switch on Leaderboards?

Leaderboards (Game Center) and Play stats are for players aged 16 and older. Under 16, nothing in the game goes online.

What do the leaderboards rank?

Today's run (out of 100), this week's runs added, all your runs added, and your empire's all-time profit. The game shows your place and the number of players; names and friends are in Apple's own Game Center pages.
